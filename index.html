Calíope - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Calíope ("the App") is a teleprompter application for recording videos. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is handled.

Data Collection

Calíope does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

The App operates entirely offline. All data (scripts, settings, preferences) is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any third party.

Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions solely for its core functionality. All processing happens on your device.

  • Camera: Used to record video with the teleprompter overlay. Video is processed locally on your device.
  • Microphone: Used to record audio alongside video. Audio data is not transmitted externally.
  • Save videos to your library (add-only): Used to save the videos you record to your device's photo library when you tap "Save". The App requests add-only (write) access and does not read or browse your existing library.
  • Select a photo (virtual backgrounds): When you choose to set a photo from your gallery as a virtual background, the system photo picker lets you select a single image. The App receives only the image you pick; it does not scan or access the rest of your library. The selected image is processed and stored locally on your device.

Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. This includes:

  • Scripts you create or import
  • App settings and preferences
  • Language preference

No data is synced to the cloud or shared with third parties.

Analytics & Tracking

Calíope does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not collect usage data, crash reports, or device identifiers.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The App is rated 4+ and does not contain age-restricted content.

Data Deletion

All your data is stored locally on your device. To delete all app data, simply uninstall the App from your device. You can also delete individual scripts within the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
